What is M3U and Xtream?

M3U is a file format that contains a list of streaming links. It looks like this: http://provider.com:8080/get.php?username=xxx&password=xxx&type=m3u_plus

Xtream Codes is another format using 3 fields: Server URL, Username, and Password. Example: Server: http://provider.com:8080 Username: myuser123 Password: mypass123

Both are supported by KAMEL PRO. You get these from your legal IPTV provider, not from us.

Method 1: Add via Website (Easiest)

Step 1: Open KAMEL PRO on your TV

Install KAMEL PRO from Google Play or Mediafire link on your Android TV. Open the app. You will see your MAC address (like AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF) and a 6-digit ID.

Step 2: Go to kamel-pro.com

On your phone or computer, go to kamel-pro.com. Enter the MAC and ID you saw on TV and press Login.

Step 3: Add Your Playlist

After login, you will see two buttons:

  • Add M3U: Click it, paste your full M3U link (must start with http:// or https://), and click Save.
  • Add Xtream: Click it, enter Server URL, Username, Password, and click Save.

Your playlist will appear instantly on your TV. No need to restart. You can add multiple playlists.

Method 2: Add via QR Code (Fast)

On the login page, click "Scan QR from TV". Allow camera permission. Point your phone camera at the QR code displayed on the TV app. The MAC and ID will fill automatically, then press Login. Then add your M3U as above.

Troubleshooting - Playlist Not Working?

1. Check your link: Open your M3U link in VLC on computer. If it doesn't work there, the problem is your provider, not KAMEL PRO.

2. MAC format: Must be like AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF with colons.

3. Internet: Make sure your TV box is connected to internet.

4. Special characters: If your Xtream password has special characters like @ or &, ask your provider for a simpler password.
