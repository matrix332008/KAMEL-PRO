Many people confuse IPTV Player and IPTV Provider. This confusion causes legal problems. In this article, we explain clearly what is an IPTV media player, how it works, and why KAMEL PRO is a 100% legal media player just like VLC, MX Player, or Windows Media Player.

What is an IPTV Media Player?

An IPTV Media Player is a software application that can play video streams from M3U or Xtream links that YOU provide. It does NOT contain any channels, movies, or content inside it. Think of it like a DVD player: the DVD player itself is empty, you must insert your own DVD. Same for KAMEL PRO - the app is empty, you insert your own legal playlist link.

Examples of legal media players: VLC, MX Player, Kodi (official), KAMEL PRO, TiviMate (player mode), IPTV Smarters Player.

What is an IPTV Provider?

An IPTV Provider is a company that sells you access to TV channels via internet. They give you an M3U link or Xtream login. They own the servers and the content rights (or should). Example: Your local internet provider that offers TV package, or a legal OTT service in your country.
