About KAMEL PRO

KAMEL PRO is a modern, secure media player application designed to help you manage and organize your personal playlists.

Our Story

Many users have legal IPTV subscriptions from their local providers but struggle to find a simple and secure player for their Smart TV. KAMEL PRO was born to solve this - a tool that works like VLC, just a player, nothing else, but with modern features like MAC activation and QR login.

What We Do

Media Player Only

KAMEL PRO is a media player. We do not provide, sell, or host any video content, TV channels, movies, or subscriptions.

Playlist Management

Easily add and organize your M3U and Xtream playlist links. All playlists are stored securely and synced to your activated device.

Multi-Device Activation

Activate your TV using MAC address and ID. Manage all playlists from kamel-pro.com.

User Privacy First

We do not track what you watch. We do not store your playlist content. Your privacy is our priority.

Important Notice

Users are solely responsible for the content they add. KAMEL PRO does not provide any media content. All playlists must be legally obtained.
