Privacy Policy - KAMEL PRO

Last updated: July 25, 2026

1. Introduction

KAMEL PRO is a media player application only. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect or store:

  • Any IPTV playlists, M3U links, or Xtream codes you enter
  • Any media content you play
  • Your viewing history or streaming activity

We only store:

  • MAC address and Activation ID you provide to link your device
  • Technical data needed for the app to function (device activation status, last seen)

3. How We Use Your Data

MAC address and Activation ID are used solely to activate your device and sync playlists you add. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices or Google Ads Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

All data is transmitted securely using HTTPS encryption. We implement security measures to protect your information.

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
